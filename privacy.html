Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Fremont Physical Therapy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, and email address when you contact us directly.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on the site, and browser type, collected anonymously through analytics tools.
  • Communications — messages or inquiries you send to us via email or phone.

We do not collect protected health information (PHI) through this website. PHI is handled exclusively through our HIPAA-compliant patient management system. Please see our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices for details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule appointments
  • To improve our website and patient experience
  • To send appointment reminders when you have opted in

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the site.

Third-Party Links

Our website links to third-party services including our patient portal (Spry) and Google Maps. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. For sensitive medical information, please contact us directly by phone.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
