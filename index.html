Do I need a referral to start physical therapy? +
In Idaho, you can see a physical therapist without a physician referral for up to 42 days (direct access). After that period, a referral may be required depending on your insurance.
How long is a typical appointment? +
Initial evaluations typically run 60 minutes. Follow-up visits are usually 45–60 minutes depending on your treatment plan. Your therapist will let you know what to expect at your first visit.
What should I bring to my first appointment? +
Bring your insurance card, a photo ID, any relevant imaging (X-rays, MRI reports), and a list of medications. If you received a referral, bring that too. Wear comfortable clothing that allows access to the injured area.
How do I pay my bill online? +
To pay your bill, give us a call at 208-624-4008 or email us at fremontpt@gmail.com and our billing team will take care of you.
Do you accept my insurance? +
We accept nearly all major insurance plans including Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Regence, SelectHealth, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Worker's Compensation, and more. Call us to verify your specific plan.
Is this the same clinic as before? +
Yes! Fremont Physical Therapy has been proudly serving the St. Anthony area for years. We've updated our look and expanded our services under new ownership, but our commitment to the community remains the same. Welcome back!
